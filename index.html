Why it is grey and white

Look at the movie again and the design is a deliberate argument. The suburb is candy pastel, every house a different sugary shade, every lawn identical. The castle on the hill, and Edward himself, are black and charcoal and pale. Burton puts the colorful world and the monochrome world next to each other and lets you decide which one is actually warmer.

That is also why this page looks the way it does. Grey, white, and a little candlelight.

Where the snow comes from

The film is told as a bedtime story by an elderly Kim to her granddaughter, who asks why it snows. The answer is Edward, still alone in his castle, carving ice sculptures, and the shavings drift down over the town. It is the reason the movie feels like a fairy tale and not just a drama.

Nothing here is scary. It is sad in the way that good stories are.
